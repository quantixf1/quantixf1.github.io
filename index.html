The Origin

ABOUT QUANTIX

Quantix is born from the fusion of two concepts: Quantum — representing unmatched speed and precision — and Matrix — embodying structured, intelligent systems. We engineer with purpose. Every decision is driven by data and refined through iteration.

At Regional Finals 2026, we placed 15th out of 150 teams and were awarded the prestigious Resilience Award — recognition of our determination and composure under pressure. We now head to National Finals 2026.

F1 in Schools is the world's largest STEM competition — students design, manufacture and race miniature CO₂-powered cars (20cm) against the clock. Teams are judged on engineering, portfolio, verbal presentation, and team identity. Quantix represents India on the national stage.

AERODYNAMICS

CFD-optimised body geometry engineered for minimal drag. Every curve is deliberate — shaped by simulation, refined through iteration.

02 ⚗️

MATERIALS

Precision-engineered balsa and carbon composite construction. Maximum stiffness, minimum weight — every gram is a performance decision.

03 ⚡

PROPULSION
