The Machine

ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

01 🌀

AERODYNAMICS

CFD-optimised body geometry engineered for a minimal drag coefficient. Every curve is deliberate — shaped by simulation, refined by iteration.

02 ⚗️

MATERIALS

Precision-engineered balsa and carbon composite construction. Maximum stiffness, minimum weight — every gram is a performance decision.

03 ⚡

PROPULSION

CO₂ cartridge system tuned for maximum thrust-to-weight ratio. Launch performance that hits the 0.04s target with room to spare.

04 📐

DESIGN

CAD-driven iterative development with virtual wind tunnel testing. 15+ revisions to arrive at geometry balancing speed, stability, and precision.

Season Progress

SEASON ROADMAP

From the first sketch to the start line — every phase of the Quantix 2025–26 campaign, engineered with intent.

Completed — Oct 2025

Concept & Team Identity

Team formation and naming finalised. Visual identity system created — logo, colour palette, and typography. School registration with F1 in Schools India confirmed. Brand ethos "Driven by Precision" established.

Completed — Dec 2025

Research & CFD Simulation

200+ hours of aerodynamic research and computational fluid dynamics simulation. 15 design revisions completed. Drag coefficient target locked at Cd 0.18. Material specification — balsa core with carbon composite overlay — finalised.

Completed — Feb 2026

Manufacturing & Build

Car fabricated using CNC-cut balsa and hand-laid carbon composite. Component testing completed across three prototypes. Final car mass achieved within target specification. CO₂ propulsion system validated.

In Progress — Now

Regional Finals Preparation

Final launch testing and performance validation underway. Engineering portfolio, verbal presentation, and car display all in final review. Sponsor documentation and identity portfolio complete.

Upcoming — 10 May 2026

Regional Finals

Quantix competes at Regional Finals. Judging covers car performance, engineering portfolio, verbal presentation, and team identity. Target: qualify for National Finals and achieve a top-3 finish.

Upcoming — Jun 2026

National Finals

National stage competition. All F1 in Schools judging categories. Quantix's ambition: podium finish and an International qualifier berth — proving India can engineer at the highest level of competitive motorsport design.
